applepie

Apple pie is a classic dessert made with a flaky pastry crust and a sweet apple filling flavored with cinnamon and sugar.
It is best served warm and is a favorite for holidays and family gatherings.

Ingredients
  • 2 pie crusts
  • 6 apples, peeled and sliced
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 2 tablespoons flour
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice
  • 1 egg (for egg wash, optional)
Steps
  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Peel, core, and slice the apples.
  3. Mix the apples with sugar, cinnamon, flour, and lemon juice.
  4. Place one pie crust into a pie dish.
  5. Pour the apple mixture into the crust and dot with butter.
  6. Cover with the second pie crust and seal the edges.
  7. Cut a few small slits in the top crust to let steam escape.
  8. Brush the top with beaten egg if desired.
  9. Bake for 45–50 minutes, until the crust is golden brown.
  10. Let the pie cool for at least 20 minutes before serving.
