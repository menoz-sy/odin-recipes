chickenpie

Chicken pie is a comforting dish made with tender chicken, vegetables, and a creamy sauce,
all wrapped in a golden, flaky pastry crust. It's a delicious and satisfying meal that's perfect for lunch or dinner.

Ingredients

  • 2 pie crusts
  • 2 cups cooked chicken, shredded
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables (peas and carrots)
  • 1 small onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 tablespoons flour
  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • 1 egg (for egg wash, optional)

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
  2. Melt the butter in a pan and cook the onion and garlic until soft.
  3. Stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute.
  4. Gradually add the chicken broth and milk, stirring until the sauce thickens.
  5. Add the shredded chicken, mixed vegetables, salt, and pepper. Mix well.
  6. Place one pie crust into a pie dish.
  7. Pour the chicken filling into the crust.
  8. Cover with the second pie crust and seal the edges.
  9. Cut a few small slits in the top crust to let steam escape.
  10. Brush the top with beaten egg if desired.
  11. Bake for 35–40 minutes, until the crust is golden brown.
  12. Let the pie cool for 10 minutes before serving.
