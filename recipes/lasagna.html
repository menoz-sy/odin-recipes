Lasagna

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, rich meat sauce, creamy béchamel or cheese,
and melted mozzarella. It's a hearty, flavorful meal that's perfect for family dinners and special occasions.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Tomato sauce
  • Tomato paste
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Egg
  • Olive oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Italian seasoning
  • Fresh basil or parsley

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions and drain them.
  3. Heat olive oil in a pan and cook the onion and garlic until soft.
  4. Add the ground beef and cook until browned.
  5. Stir in the tomato sauce, tomato paste, and Italian seasoning. Simmer for 15 minutes.
  6. In a bowl, mix the ricotta cheese, egg, and half of the Parmesan cheese.
  7. Spread a layer of meat sauce in a baking dish.
  8. Add a layer of lasagna noodles, then the ricotta mixture, meat sauce, and mozzarella cheese.
  9. Repeat the layers until all the ingredients are used.
  10. Top with the remaining mozzarella and Parmesan cheese.
  11. Bake for 35–40 minutes, until the cheese is melted and golden brown.
  12. Let the lasagna rest for 10 minutes before slicing and serving.
